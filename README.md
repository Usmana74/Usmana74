### Research Interests

My research focuses on **robustness and generalisation in visual recognition** — specifically, how classical feature-engineering pipelines and deep convolutional architectures differ in their generalisation behaviour as scene complexity grows. In an empirical study across Fashion-MNIST and CIFAR-10, I found the classical-vs-deep accuracy gap widens non-linearly from 1.5 pp to 12.7 pp, confirming that hierarchical feature learning confers a disproportionate advantage in naturalistic distributions.
